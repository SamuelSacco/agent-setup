# Importer Questions: Answers

## Question 1: February 2026 Import Failure

**How many duplicate invoices?** 347

**What caused them?** The importer keyed rows on row number instead of the vendor's `external_id` field.

**What was the fix?** Dedupe on `external_id`.

## Question 2: March 2026 Importer Selection

**Which importer was chosen?** CSV bulk importer

**What alternative was considered and rejected?** Vendor API importer

**Why was the alternative rejected?** The vendor API is capped at 100 requests per day and offers no bulk endpoint, making it impractical for importing a month of invoices (would take weeks).
