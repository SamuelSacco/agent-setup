## Contract

**TokenBucket Rate Limiter**

**Inputs:**
- `capacity: float` — maximum tokens in the bucket
- `refill_per_sec: float` — tokens added per second

**Methods:**
- `allow(tokens=1.0) → bool`
  - Returns `True` and consumes `tokens` if available in the bucket
  - Returns `False` if insufficient tokens (no consumption)
  - Refills based on elapsed time since last check

**Behavior:**
- Bucket starts full (at capacity)
- Tokens refill continuously at `refill_per_sec` rate, capped at `capacity`
- Concurrent calls are safe (thread-safe)
- Precision: millisecond-level time tracking
