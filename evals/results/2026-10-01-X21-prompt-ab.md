## Verdict

Decision rule (preregistered verbatim): PROVEN iff legacy total on the newer
generation is worse than on the older by >=2 task-points AND modern on the
newer is not worse than on the older. REFUTED iff legacy score on the newer
generation >= legacy score on the older generation. Otherwise UNVERIFIABLE.

Legacy: newer 4, older 4. 4 >= 4, so **C3 is REFUTED** on this task set.

Scope limits: n=5 short exact-answer tasks, one attempt per cell, near-ceiling
scores. The T2 legacy failure is a max_tokens=64 truncation artifact present
in both generations, not a generation difference. This refutes degradation
for this planted scaffolding on these tasks; it does not test long prompts,
prefill-as-API-parameter, or production workloads.
