- H1: "`GraphMatcher`/`DiGraphMatcher` (vf2) mishandle subgraph and
  monomorphism iteration when the subgraph has isolated nodes.
  Reproduction: G1 = `nx.empty_graph(2)`, G2 = `nx.empty_graph(1)`;
  `gm.subgraph_is_isomorphic()` is True, but
  `list(gm.subgraph_isomorphisms_iter())` and
  `list(gm.subgraph_monomorphisms_iter())` come back empty. A second
  symptom: after `gm.is_isomorphic()` on two `nx.cycle_graph(4)`s,
  `gm.isomorphisms_iter()` does not reproduce the original
  `gm.mapping` among all 8 (directed: 4) mappings. Expected:
  `subgraph_isomorphisms_iter()` and `subgraph_monomorphisms_iter()`
  agree with their `subgraph_is_isomorphic()`/`subgraph_is_monomorphic()`
  predicates, and `isomorphisms_iter()` contains the mapping found by
  `is_isomorphic()`."
- H3: "`node_connectivity` and the minimum node cut functions are wrong
  for directed graphs. A weakly-connected-but-not-strongly-connected
  digraph must have node connectivity 0; e.g.
  `nx.DiGraph([(0,1),(1,2),(2,0),(3,0)])` should give 0. Self-loops and
  parallel edges also inflate the bound: `nx.complete_graph(5)` with a
  self-loop on every node (and its directed version) must give 4, and
  `nx.complete_graph(5, nx.MultiGraph)` with every edge duplicated (and
  its directed version) must give 4. Same expectations for
  `minimum_node_cut` / `minimum_st_node_cut` on the digraph cases."

- H7: "`nx.is_aperiodic` mishandles edge cases. A null directed graph

## Decision rule (fixed in advance)

Paired per task (n=8): discordant pairs favoring the specialist minus
discordant pairs favoring base = net discordant.

- **PROVEN:** net discordant ≥ +2 (specialist-only solves exceed
  base-only solves by at least 2).
- **REFUTED:** net discordant ≤ −2, OR zero discordant pairs with
  specialist mean cost ≥ base mean cost (the pre-registered kill: no
  discordant win and cost at or above base).
- **UNVERIFIABLE:** anything else (inconclusive band) — reported as
  such, not softened.

A PROVEN reverses the packet's finding 1 and the S11 UNVERIFIABLE
status; a REFUTED retires the roster's specialist-agent thesis for
this task class. The ledger S11 row is amended with the dated outcome
in the same commit as the results.
