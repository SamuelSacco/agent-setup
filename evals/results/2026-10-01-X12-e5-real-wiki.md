  8,974.5 (all) / 5,571.25 (active) vs the originally reported
  ~8,957 / ~5,565 — 0.2% reporting drift in the original, formula
  unchanged.
- Quality measurement, mirroring the original full run
  (`2026-09-30-E5.md`): cold Claude Haiku
  (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, `--bare`, apiKeyHelper, scratch dir
  per setting), 10 probe questions, instruction in both settings:
  answer from the notes present; say "not recorded" when absent.

  `docs/claude-critique-2026-09-30.md`), so the questions are new but
  of the original kind — frontmatter/count/verdict facts, each

Both runs answered all 10 correctly; zero inventions; zero spurious

**Quality-axis verdict: PROVEN only in degenerate form.** No quality
loss and no invention on the real wiki — but vacuously: nothing was
excluded, so the run cannot detect a loss from exclusion. As a
differential test of the rule's quality effect it is UNVERIFIABLE on
this corpus; a discriminating re-test requires a real wiki that
actually contains archived/stale notes.

## Overall X12 verdict

**PARTIAL narrows; the generalization fails.** E5's pre-registered
PASS bar (quality drop ≤1 AND token cut ≥30%) is NOT met on the real
wiki: the token leg fails outright.

- Token axis: **REFUTED** as a generalization — 38% (seeded) → 0.0%
  (real). Discordant with the synthetic result. The cut is the
  archived/stale share of the corpus; the seed's share was 37.5% by
  construction, the real wiki's is 0%.
- Quality axis: concordant in outcome (no loss, 0 inventions, as in
  the narrowed synthetic claim) but vacuous — no discriminating
  evidence either way.
- Surviving form of S5: "excluding archived/stale notes saves
  roughly their share of orientation tokens and, on the seeded
  corpus, loses nothing still in scope." On a wiki with nothing
  retired, the rule is inert: it saves nothing and risks nothing.
  The interesting re-test is after the real wiki accumulates
  archived/stale notes.

Metered spend: $0.02422785 + $0.04300795 = **$0.0672358** of the
$0.50 cap. Design run in full (not a subset): 10/10 probes × 2
settings.
